Lapin Puusepänverstas is a design studio and woodworking workshop based in Rauma, Finland, with a strong focus on nature and traditional craftsmanship. All products are carefully made in small batches using locally sourced materials.

Production begins with the careful selection and preparation of timber. The primary materials are locally sourced birch and oak, grown slowly in the surrounding archipelago. The wood is dried and processed at a measured pace to ensure it retains its shape and durability for generations.

All products are designed and crafted in Lappi, a rural district of Rauma, Finland. The modern production process combines 3D modeling, CNC milling, and traditional woodworking techniques. Each piece is finished by hand to ensure the final result is as authentic and natural as possible.

The company is guided by the principles of sustainable and local production. Lapin Puusepänverstas seeks to preserve the tradition of fine woodworking while highlighting the importance of the wood’s origin in Finnish design.
